Frequently asked questions

How do I install it?

Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store, open gemini.google.com, and click the blue sidebar button in the bottom-right corner.

How does Pro activation work?

After you subscribe, you receive a license key instantly on the confirmation page and by email. Paste it into the extension's options page to unlock Pro.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Your subscription is billed monthly and you can cancel at any time from the customer portal. You keep Pro access until the end of the paid period.

Do you store my conversations?

No. Folders, chat titles and preferences are stored locally in your browser. We never collect or transmit your conversation content.
