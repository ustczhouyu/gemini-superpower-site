Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Gemini Superpower ("we", "us", "the extension") handles information when you use our Chrome browser extension and this website.

1. Information we do NOT collect

Gemini Superpower is designed to work entirely inside your browser. We do not collect, store, transmit or sell:

  • Your Gemini conversation content or prompts
  • Your chat messages, responses or attachments
  • Your browsing history outside of gemini.google.com
  • Your Google account credentials

2. Information stored locally on your device

The extension stores the following data locally in your browser (Chrome local storage), and it never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it:

  • Names of the folders you create
  • Titles and links of the chats you choose to save
  • Your extension preferences (e.g. theme, sidebar state)
  • Your license key, if you have purchased Pro

You can delete this data at any time by uninstalling the extension or clearing your browser storage.

3. Payment information

When you subscribe to Pro, payments are processed by our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments. We never see or store your full card number. Dodo Payments handles all payment data, tax compliance and invoicing under their own privacy policy.

4. License validation

To verify your Pro subscription, the extension sends your license key to a secure validation service. Only the license key itself is transmitted — no personal data, no conversation content, and no usage statistics are sent.

5. Permissions we request and why

  • storage — to save your folders, saved chats and preferences locally
  • downloads — to save exported chats as files to your computer
  • access to gemini.google.com — to inject the sidebar and read the current chat title

We request no permission beyond what is required for these features.

6. Third parties

We do not share, sell or transfer your data to any third party, except the payment processor (Dodo Payments) strictly necessary to complete a purchase you initiate.

7. Children's privacy

The extension is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
