Refund Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Summary: If Gemini Superpower Pro doesn't work for you, email us within 14 days of purchase and we'll refund your most recent payment — no questions asked.

1. 14-day money-back guarantee

We want you to be satisfied with Gemini Superpower Pro. If you are not happy with your purchase for any reason, you may request a full refund within 14 days of the initial purchase or the most recent renewal.

2. How to request a refund

Send an email to ustczhouyu0226@163.com with:

  • The email address used for the purchase, and
  • Your license key (if available)

We aim to respond within 2 business days. Approved refunds are processed by our Merchant of Record, Dodo Payments, and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

3. Subscriptions and cancellation

  • Pro is billed monthly and renews automatically until cancelled.
  • You can cancel at any time from the customer portal (link provided in your purchase email).
  • After cancelling, you keep Pro access until the end of the current paid period.
  • Cancellation alone does not trigger a refund; refunds are handled under the 14-day guarantee above.

4. Exceptions

We may decline a refund in cases of:

  • Abuse of the refund policy (repeated purchase-and-refund cycles)
  • Fraudulent or unauthorized transactions
  • Requests made more than 14 days after the payment in question

5. Defective product

If the extension is materially broken and we cannot fix it within a reasonable time, you are entitled to a full refund regardless of the 14-day window.
